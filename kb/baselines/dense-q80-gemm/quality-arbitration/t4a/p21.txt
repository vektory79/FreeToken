### reasoning
Today is Wednesday. I need to find what day it will be in 100 days.

100 mod 7 = 100 ÷ 7 = 14 remainder 2 (since 14 × 7 = 98, 100 - 98 = 2).

So 100 days is 14 full weeks plus 2 extra days.

Wednesday + 2 days = Thursday (1), Friday (2).

So it will be Friday.
