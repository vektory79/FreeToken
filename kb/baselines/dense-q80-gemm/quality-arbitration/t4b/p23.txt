### reasoning
The user asks which is larger: 2^10 or 10^3, with a short justification.

2^10 = 1024
10^3 = 1000

So 2^10 = 1024 > 1000 = 10^3.

Short justification: compute both directly. 2^10 = 2·2·2·2·2·2·2·2·2·2 = 1024. 10^3 = 1000. Since 1024 > 1000, 2^10 is larger.
